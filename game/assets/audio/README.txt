Original procedural garden audio, generated locally for Backyard Brave.
13 distinct synthesized sound effects: slingshot seed, soft/shell hits, bug
defeat, roll rustle, scout hurt, spit, charge warning, seed pickup, berry heal,
guard block, upgrade and boss arrival. No sampled or third-party recordings.
Camp Garden: 24-second stereo plucked melody and bass.
Backyard Adventure: 16-second stereo variation with light wooden percussion.
Music and sound controls are independent; existing preferences are preserved.
Switch Music ON in Settings to hear the new tracks. Music pauses with menus
that pause combat. Cues are rate-limited; common sounds have slight pitch
variation without consuming gameplay randomness.
Generated with work/create-garden-audio.py (NumPy synthesis, PCM WAV).
