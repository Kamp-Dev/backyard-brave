Backyard Brave support

Developed by Xevajius.

For help, contact xevapps@gmail.com. Include your device, game version, what happened, and a screenshot if useful. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or purchase tokens.

Saved progress

Progress is stored on your device. Purchase restoration does not restore stages, mastery, or Acorn progress. Avoid deleting the app or clearing browser data while troubleshooting. Local backups currently remain in the game’s save folder; they are not cloud backups.

Outfits and purchases

The browser development build offers outfit previews; checkout is unavailable. In the Google Play release, use the same Google account that purchased your outfit and select Restore in the wardrobe. Pending payments do not unlock outfits until Google confirms completion. Outfits do not unlock characters: Poppy still requires clearing Stage 15.

Refunds

Purchases made through Google Play follow its refund process. Visit Google Play refund help. You can also contact us with the product name and order reference. Never send the purchase token. Refunded cosmetics return to the original outfit after ownership refresh; gameplay progress stays intact.

Accessibility

Settings includes reduced effects, reduced flashes, damage-number visibility, joystick visibility, sound controls and battery saver.
