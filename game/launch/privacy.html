Privacy policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026. Backyard Brave is developed by Xevajius. Contact: xevapps@gmail.com.

Game data on your device

Your scout name, settings, stages, mastery, lifetime totals and outfit choices are saved locally. The game does not require a game account and does not upload this progression to a cloud save service. Your local player ID is used for troubleshooting, not advertising.

Purchases

Checkout is disabled in the browser development build. When purchases are enabled in the Google Play release, Google processes payments. The game sends the purchased product identifier and Google purchase token over HTTPS to our verification service. That service checks ownership with Google, acknowledges completed purchases and returns a signed ownership record. It stores a hashed token, product identifier, purchase status and last verification time for up to 90 days after the last check. Raw tokens are not stored in the service’s ledger. Receipts and signed ownership records remain on your device to support restoration and offline access. We do not receive your payment card details.

Advertising and analytics

There are no advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs in this build. We do not sell your data or use your gameplay for targeted advertising. Website hosting and network providers may process IP addresses and technical connection information to deliver the game. Google Play also processes data under its own policies.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include. We use this to respond and troubleshoot. Email is handled through Gmail. Do not send sensitive information that is not needed for support. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence or verification records associated with your purchase.

Your choices

You can change your scout name and settings in the game. Removing app data deletes local progression; there is currently no cloud progression recovery. Restoring purchases recovers eligible cosmetics through the store, not gameplay saves. You can play the core game without buying anything.

Changes

We will update this page before introducing advertising, accounts, cloud saves or additional data collection. Store privacy disclosures must match the released version.
