Backyard Brave - parent data and security operations
DRAFT: to be implemented and verified before checkout is enabled.
Owner: XEV APPS developer/account owner. Contact: xevapps@gmail.com.

Scope and purpose
The intended audience includes under-13 players and older players. Apply the same data-minimizing gameplay defaults to all players. No ads, profiling, required online accounts or chat are part of this release design. Store validation is solely for delivering/restoring cosmetic purchases. No sales/marketing reuse of receipts or support messages is authorized.

Current implementation vs outstanding operations
- Verifier accepts product_id/token only. Google processes payments. The verifier checks ownership and acknowledges; only digest/product/state/check time enter its ledger.
- Ledger rows older than 90 days are removable by maintenance.py and pruned on verification. Daily-or-more-frequent scheduling and failure alerting are NOT deployed. The 90-day necessity and resulting deletion window need review before the public policy is finalized.
- Signed ownership expires after 90 days; this is separate from server retention. Receipt cache and progression live on the device. No cloud progression recovery is offered.
- Hosting logs, database backups and Gmail have separate controls. Record their actual retention, deletion, subprocessors, locations and written privacy/security assurances before launch. Do not assert deletion of Google-held financial records.

Before production
Document the final vendor/SDK inventory and review child-directed usage terms. Inspect the release bundle manifest and runtime network traffic. Provision least-privilege service credentials in secret storage; public signing key only in the game; TLS mandatory; ingress body limits/rate limits; body/query logging disabled. Limit admin access and use MFA. Define patching and key-rotation procedures. Monitor aggregate health without receipts or child identifiers. Exercise failed-storage and acknowledgement retry paths.

Parent requests
Support page asks adults to handle inquiries and avoid child's real name, birthdate, photos, identity documents or purchase tokens. First determine which system holds the requested information. Local-only progression cannot be remotely looked up by the developer. Use a proportionate, privacy-preserving authority/record-matching procedure approved for the final service before disclosing or deleting anything. This procedure is currently an OPEN RELEASE BLOCKER; an email claiming parenthood is not sufficient by itself.
After verification: record only minimum request status, handle access/deletion through authorized operator tools, separately process mail/logs/backups, and explain any lawful retention and store limitations. delete-verified-hash is only a ledger tool; it is not a refund or complete erasure across providers. A later store restore may create a fresh verification record. Close and remove request records under the approved retention policy.
If a child emails personal information: do not add them to marketing, solicit additional information, or create an ongoing child profile. Follow the reviewed response/retention procedure and applicable consent/exception; escalate uncertainty to qualified counsel.

Incident readiness
Assign a contact and response procedure for credential exposure or unexpected collection: disable checkout/affected collection, secure credentials, assess affected data/providers, preserve minimum necessary investigation evidence, and obtain advice on notice obligations. Re-test after changing an SDK, host, data field or audience feature.

Required review
Determine applicability of COPPA exceptions or verifiable parental consent for each actual collection purpose, and regional child/privacy requirements for intended distribution. Google Play purchase approval and a parent UI are not substitutes for this review. No age/consent checkbox or math question is asserted to prove parental consent.
